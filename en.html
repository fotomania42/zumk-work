FAQ

How far in advance should I book?
As soon as your travel dates are set. The badge at the top of this page shows whether I'm taking bookings for this month.
What if it rains on the day?
We move the shoot to another day that works for both of us. There's no cancellation. We just find a new date. If you're only in Korea for a few days, leave a spare day in your plans if you can.
Can you shoot outside Seoul?
Yes. Prices cover the Seoul metropolitan area (Seoul, Gyeonggi, Incheon). Anywhere else adds a travel fee based on distance.
Do I need to bring a handler?
Not for a regular shoot at a quiet location. I take care of the basics while shooting. For crowded places or a full convention day, the con companion plan covers handling too.
Do I get RAW files?
Unedited previews are available to help you pick favorites. RAW files aren't included by default. They're available as an add-on that includes copyright and commercial rights.
