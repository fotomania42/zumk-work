FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions.

When will I get my photos?
On the day of the shoot, I edit and send you the best 3–4 A-cuts first. The full edited set follows in about a month.
I'm worried about being photographed with my head off.
I never take photos while your head is off. I put the camera down during breaks, so feel free to rest.
Is there a time limit?
The photoshoot is priced per session, so I don't count the hours. I stay and shoot until the meetup ends.
What if it rains or I'm not feeling well?
We don't cancel the booking. We agree on another date that works for both of us and shoot then. If anything comes up, just let me know.
Can you shoot outside the Seoul area?
Yes. All prices are based on the Seoul metropolitan area, and shoots elsewhere add a travel fee based on distance. Tell me the location when you get in touch and I'll give you the details.
Who pays for studio rental or location fees?
Studio rental and entrance fees for outdoor locations are not included in the price. You'll need to book and pay for them yourself.
Do I need to bring a supporter?
Not unless the location is crowded and busy. I know how tough suiting can be, so basic care and support come with every shoot.
Do I get the original files?
Unedited originals are available on request, but please use them only to pick your favorites. RAW files aren't included by default. If you'd like to keep them, they're a separate paid option that includes copyright and commercial use rights.
